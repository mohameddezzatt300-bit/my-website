( 01 · What we walked into )

Background & challenge.

01 / 05

01

A brand-new niche

Took over in July 2024 in a category the brand was pioneering — unique and unfamiliar to the market.

02

No direct competitors

A blessing and a curse: no benchmarks to learn from, no competitor audiences to borrow.

03

Premium pricing

2,000–6,000 EGP per unit meant an upscale buyer, CPMs above 150 EGP and long consideration windows.

04

Unstable order costs

CAC swung widely. We stabilised it before scaling, not during.

05

In-stream fraud

In-stream placements generated fake orders. We shut them off and moved budget — a 2-day call, not a 2-week debate.

( 02 · What we had going for us )

Key advantages.

01 / 05

01

High-quality content

Executed flawlessly — the #1 driver. Great creative covered a lot of gaps elsewhere.

02

Client support

Access, assets and fast decisions. An aligned client is worth more than any framework.

03

Variety

Products for every space, indoor and outdoor, which widened the creative surface dramatically.

04

Customisation

Custom designs at higher prices — a natural basket-size lever and a reason to come back.

05

Many occasions

Different mats for different occasions built repeat purchases into the product.

( 03 · The playbook )

The strategy.

01 / 04

01

Daily testing discipline

Audiences, interests and creatives tested on a steady rhythm — tests don't run themselves.

02

Platform focus

Budget went where results were reliable, not spread thin for the look of being everywhere.

03

Advantage+ audiences

Advantage+ shopping campaigns outran hand-built audiences once we trusted the algorithm.

04

ABO multi-ad, flexible

No single structure: flexible ABO multi-ad campaigns were the workhorse of the account.
