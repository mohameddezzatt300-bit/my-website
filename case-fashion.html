( 01 · Starting position )

The challenge.

01 / 05

01

No website at all

The brand had no website. We built one so we could own the funnel and collect first-party data.

02

Client hesitation

Leadership was hesitant about a web presence. We made the case, set expectations, and went ahead.

03

DM-only communication

Customer contact lived in DMs — no funnel, no retargeting pool, no lifetime-value visibility.

04

A hard audience to crack

A clothing-focused 35–45 audience: noisy interest stacks and fast creative fatigue.

05

UX was the bottleneck

Significant site changes made it easier for this audience to buy — it wasn't only a traffic problem.

( 02 · How we played it )

The strategy.

01 / 05

01

Trending, unique products

Led with trending, differentiated products that weren't already everywhere — not another lookalike catalogue.

02

Work with what you've got

With few images or videos available, a small designer team and smart re-cuts got the most from every asset.

03

Instagram first

Focused on Instagram given the follower base and Facebook's weak performance for this audience.

04

Upscale geo-targeting

Targeted upscale zones in Cairo, Alexandria and the North Coast to match a 2,000–6,000 EGP basket.

05

Competitor & market analysis

Positioning first, ads second — a clear angle against competitors before scaling spend.
