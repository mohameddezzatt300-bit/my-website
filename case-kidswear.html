( Case study 03 · Kidswear · COD )

Reading the delivered number, not the dashboard.

IndustryKidswear · eCom
GoalDelivered sales
MarketEgypt · COD
Catalog9,000+ products

A large Egyptian kidswear brand: 9,000+ products, 40+ stores, and about 9 in 10 orders paid cash on delivery. Meta said 7.8× ROAS. The cash that actually arrived said 4.6× — so we tied every campaign to delivered orders and managed to that number.

( 02 · The numbers behind the numbers )

What we found.

01 / 04

01

Delivered ROAS per campaign

UTMs → Shopify → delivery status, campaign by campaign.

The real top category

Underwear: 30% of revenue, and it had never been advertised.

Checkout friction

Cash on delivery was listed last, though 89% of orders used it.

The market's price

A bid ladder showed where the market cleared: ROAS 10 at a cost per order near 120 EGP.
